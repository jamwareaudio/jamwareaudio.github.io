TouchXY Coming soon

Your trackpad as an XY controller for the selected device in Live.

Turn your trackpad into an XY pad for the device selected in Live. Press ⌃⌥T and the whole trackpad becomes the pad: X and Y control the two most useful parameters of the device. Select another device and the pad follows, with nothing to map. Live keeps the keyboard, so the space bar still starts and stops playback. Each axis sends 14-bit MIDI, so a slow filter sweep stays smooth.

No window needed

TouchXY lives in the JamWare Hub menu with your other JamWare apps. It starts with no window and has no menu bar icon of its own, so it takes no space on screen.

Press ⌃⌥T anywhere, with Live in front, and your trackpad controls the selected device. Sweep, then press ⌃⌥T again to get your pointer back. While it is on, the pointer stays still and taps and clicks do nothing, so you cannot change anything in Live by accident.

Open the pad window only when you want to drag with the mouse or map two CCs by hand: choose TouchXY in the Hub menu, or press ⌃⌥X.

Features

  • Your trackpad is the controller. Press ⌃⌥T and the whole trackpad maps to the whole pad. The pointer stays still, taps and clicks are ignored, and Live keeps the keyboard, so the space bar still runs the transport.
  • Follows the selected device. On an Auto Filter, X is Frequency and Y is Resonance. Reverb, Echo, Operator, EQ Eight, Compressor and many more have their own pairs. Anything else, third-party plugins included, gets its first two continuous parameters.
  • Live stays in front. With focus lock on, dragging the pad does not take focus from Live.
  • MIDI map mode. Turn device mode off and X and Y are two plain CCs on channel 16. Map them to anything with Live’s MIDI Map Mode, or in any other DAW.
  • 14-bit on both axes. 16384 steps instead of 128. Live reads the fine and coarse pair as one control.
  • Stays out of the way. It usually runs with no window. ⌃⌥X or the JamWare Hub menu shows the pad, and it floats above full-screen Live.
  • No Control Surface slot of its own. It shares the JamWare slot with MutationStation and Chordinator.

Requirements

macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon, and Ableton Live for device mode.

Trackpad mode needs Accessibility permission, because it holds the pointer still while you sweep. macOS asks for it the first time.

Intel Macs: beta. The app is built for Intel too and should work, but it has not been tested on an Intel Mac yet.

Signed and notarised for macOS, so it opens with a normal double-click, with no security warning.
