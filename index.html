JamWare Audio — The Companion Suite

Small tools that do one loud thing well.

Mac apps built around the parts of a session that software usually leaves you to do by hand: mutating a bassline into a hundred, triggering chords from single keys, seeing what you are actually hearing, and deciding what a knob does. MutationStation

Acid lines without programming every note.

Give it a line and it mutates it across pitch, rhythm, accent, slide, octave and length into fresh variations you can audition, edit and drag straight into your DAW. Lock the notes that already work, in the Elektron tradition, and re-roll everything else as often as you like.

Chordinator

Spectrl — macOS app

Spectrl

A spectrum analyser for everything your Mac plays.

Live spectrum, oscilloscope and loudness metering with musical note mapping, tonal-balance targets and WAV capture. It taps your default output through Core Audio, so it hears your DAW without a plugin on the master, and without touching your screen or microphone.

  • macOS 14.4+ · Apple Silicon
  • Core Audio process tap
  • No plugin required

MidiMirror

Build what your MIDI controller does, without writing Python.

Decide what every knob, fader and key reaches in Ableton Live, then generate a real Remote Script for your controller. Custom modes, two jobs on one key, a drop that snaps the whole panel back, and a second-screen mirror that labels every control as it moves.

About

Why they all look the same

JamWare Audio tools are built to sit next to your DAW for hours without getting in the way. They share one design language on purpose: a brushed faceplate, a dark inset screen for anything you actually read, and exactly one accent colour per app doing all the signalling.

That last rule is the whole system. MutationStation is red, Chordinator amber, Spectrl blue, MidiMirror red-orange, one colour each, so a glance at a window tells you which tool you are in, and so no app ever has two colours competing to mean "important".

Everything is small, does one job, and stays out of the master chain. Nothing phones home.
