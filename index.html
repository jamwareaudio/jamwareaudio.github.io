The apps

MutationStation · macOS app

MutationStation

Lock the notes that work, re-roll/mutate the rest, and chase acid lines you would not have written.

Give it a line and it mutates it across pitch, rhythm, accent, slide, octave and length into fresh variations you can audition, edit and drag straight into your DAW. Lock the notes that already work, in the Elektron tradition, and re-roll everything else as often as you like. Play notes straight into it from a keyboard or a DAW track, pull a clip in from Ableton Live and push it back out as one, and draw up to eight modulation lanes that stream live or bake into a Session clip as automation.

Chordinator · macOS app

Chordinator

128 chords under one keybed, and a chord generator to help you pick or follow the tonic of your bass line.

A whole chord under one key, laid out so there is no wrong note on the keybed, with dozens of harmony engines and live suggestions for where to go next. Build it out with a poly piano roll, a rhythm generator measured from real dub house and dub techno records, groove and humanize passes, and eight modulation lanes that land in Live as clip envelopes.

Get it on Gumroad Details

Spectrl · macOS app

Spectrl

Spectrum, key, note frequencies, tonal balance and a system-audio sampler in one window.

Five tools reading one signal: live spectrum with musical note mapping, a Scale panel that listens for ten seconds and names the key, loudness metering, an oscilloscope and tonal-balance targets. It taps your default output through Core Audio, so it hears your DAW without a plugin on the master, and records straight to WAV with nothing to route.

MidiMirror · Remote Script + app

MidiMirror

A mirror for your hardware MIDI controller: see the exact values of your knobs, and customise what each one does.

Decide what every knob, fader and key reaches in Ableton Live, then generate a real Remote Script for your controller. Custom modes, two jobs on one key, a drop that snaps the whole panel back, and a second-screen mirror that labels every control as it moves.

Arranger · in development

Arranger

Session loops into a full arrangement, from a template.

Describe the song, as Intro 8, Verse 16, Chorus 16, and say which roles play in which section. Arranger takes each track's Session clip and lays it out across the Arrangement timeline, tiling MIDI and audio to fill every section it belongs in.

About

Why they all look the same

JamWare Audio tools are built to sit next to your DAW for hours without getting in the way. They share one design language on purpose: a brushed faceplate, a dark inset screen for anything you actually read, and exactly one accent colour per app doing all the signalling.

That last rule is the whole system. MutationStation is red, Chordinator amber, Spectrl blue, MidiMirror red-orange, one colour each, so a glance at a window tells you which tool you are in, and so no app ever has two colours competing to mean "important".

Everything is small, does one job, and stays out of the master chain. Nothing phones home.
